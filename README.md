A Make.fun micro bounty entry: a single one-line 1-star review written by a
houseplant about its human owner. The plant's grievance: its owner waters it
with leftover cold coffee, leaving it jittery, wide awake at 3 a.m., and
still somehow dying of thirst.

`main.py` writes the review to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
